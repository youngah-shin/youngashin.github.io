Present

PDP008 · Privacy by Design

Korea University, Graduate School of Privacy & Data Protection · 2 credits · Korean

Privacy-by-design principles and strategies, usable security and privacy, and case-study analysis. The course asks working professionals to treat privacy, security, and usability as constraints that must be resolved at the design stage rather than audited afterwards.

  • Foundational PbD frameworks and their translation into engineering requirements
  • Usable security and privacy, including recent results from IEEE S&P and SOUPS
  • Case-study analysis of deployed systems and regulatory incidents
Mar 2025 —
Present

PDP003 · Privacy Enhancing Technology

Korea University, Graduate School of Privacy & Data Protection · 2 credits · Korean

An introduction to modern cryptography and privacy-enhancing technologies for students without a formal cryptography background, built around what each primitive actually guarantees and what it does not.

  • Symmetric and public-key cryptography, digital signatures, and PKI
  • Zero-knowledge proofs and their use in credential and payment systems
  • Blockchain, transaction privacy, and federated learning

Teaching interests

I am prepared to teach, at both undergraduate and graduate level:

  • Introduction to cybersecurity and information security
  • Applied and modern cryptography, including provable-security methodology
  • Privacy-enhancing technologies and privacy engineering
  • Blockchain and distributed-ledger security
  • Network and system security for constrained devices

Course materials are available on request. I am happy to teach in Korean or English.
