Outreach

Through the Korea University Cyber Security Clinic Center I work with micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises that have no security staff of their own. The clinic pairs trained students with local businesses to run assessments and deliver practical remediation advice, which gives students real casework and gives firms help they would not otherwise be able to buy.

I am open to collaboration on applied cryptography, privacy-enhancing technologies, and blockchain security. Please write to yashin95@korea.ac.kr.
